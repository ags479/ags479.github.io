Common questions

  • How do I get my data on a new phone? Leave Settings → iCloud → "Sync with iCloud" on and sign in to the same Apple Account on the new phone. Or use Settings → Back up now, then Restore on the new phone.
  • My iPad doesn't show my clients. Both devices need iCloud turned on in the app and the same Apple Account. The first sync can take a few minutes on Wi-Fi.
  • How do I remove ads? Settings → Remove ads. It's a monthly subscription through Apple; cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • I already paid but see ads. Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Can my office manager use it too? The app syncs between devices on one Apple Account. Sharing between different people's accounts isn't available yet.

  • ¿Cómo paso mis datos a un teléfono nuevo? Deja activado Configuración → iCloud → "Sincronizar con iCloud" e inicia sesión con la misma cuenta de Apple. O usa Respaldar ahora y luego Restaurar.

  • Ya pagué y sigo viendo anuncios. Configuración → Restaurar compras, con la misma cuenta de Apple.
